# Verde Player

## Fully Offline

Your music stays on your device. No accounts, no streaming, no ads.

## Every Format

Supports MP3, FLAC, AAC, WAV, M4A, OGG, Opus, ALAC, and more.

## Import Your Way

Add music from Files, Wi-Fi Transfer, Share extension, or AirDrop.

## Native Experience

Browse by songs or artists, search your library, save favorites, and enjoy Lock Screen controls.
