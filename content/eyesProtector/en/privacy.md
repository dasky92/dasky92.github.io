# Privacy Policy

## Overview

EyesProtector (the “App”) is an eye-strain **habit coach** and near-work glasses **workflow companion** for Mac, iPhone, and iPad. It helps you keep look-away breaks during work hours, optionally supports dual-pair commute prompts, and may let you log subjective strain scores.

The App is **not** a medical device and does **not** diagnose, treat, or replace professional eye care.

This Privacy Policy explains who we are, what information we process, how optional **iCloud** sync and **paid** features relate to privacy, and your choices. We designed the App to minimize collection: the core habit loop works on-device without creating an account with us.

## Who We Are

The data controller for the App is **dasky92**, an individual developer based in the People’s Republic of China.

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us using the details in Contact Us below.

## Scope

This policy applies to users of the App **worldwide**, except where local law requires otherwise.

The App is intended for Apple App Store distribution under a Health & Fitness / habit positioning. Age rating is subject to Apple’s review (we expect a general audience rating such as 4+, not a children’s-directed product).

## Information We Process

We minimize collection. Depending on how you use the App, the following may apply.

### 1. On-device use (default; no account with us)

When you use the App without enabling sync, the following typically remain on your device and are **not** uploaded to servers we operate (we do **not** run a custom account backend):

- Look-away schedules, work hours, pause / off-work / mute-for-today state
- Pairing plan (single-pair vs dual-pair) and related reminder preferences
- Smart Pause preferences (for example Focus toggle, frontmost-app list on Mac)
- Symptom / strain diary entries (scores and optional notes) and look-away adherence history
- Appearance and other local settings

Uninstalling the App usually removes on-device App data, subject to how your operating system manages storage.

### 2. Optional iCloud sync (off unless you turn it on)

If you enable **iCloud Sync** in the App:

- Selected settings, reminder-related state, symptom diary, and related habit data may sync across devices signed into the **same Apple ID** using **Apple iCloud** (for example Apple’s Key-Value / iCloud-backed sync mechanisms used by the App).
- Sync is provided and hosted by **Apple**. We do **not** operate a separate cloud database of your diary on our own servers, and we **cannot** log into your iCloud account to read your synced contents.
- Turning sync **off** in the App stops new sync from the App’s side; a local copy typically remains on the device. Copies already stored in iCloud are governed by **Apple’s** iCloud retention and deletion controls (System Settings / Settings → [your name] → iCloud, and Apple’s help articles).
- iCloud availability, quotas, and regional features are determined by Apple and your Apple ID / storefront.

See [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and iCloud terms for Apple’s processing.

### 3. Permissions and system features you grant

With your permission (or as exposed by the OS), the App may use:

- **Notifications** — to deliver look-away or related habit prompts
- **Focus Status** (where available) — so Smart Pause can shelve reminders while Focus is on
- **Frontmost application information on Mac** — only to match apps you added to the Smart Pause list (for example meeting apps)

You can revoke these in system settings. Denying permission may limit related features; the rest of the App can still work.

### 4. Technical network connections

The App or your device may connect to the internet without you typing personal information into a form we host, for example:

- Checking for or downloading updates through the **Apple App Store**
- Completing **In-App Purchases / subscriptions** (if offered) via Apple
- Fetching publicly hosted legal documents (such as this policy) from **GitHub Pages**
- Communicating with **Apple iCloud** when sync is enabled

We do not use these connections to build advertising profiles about you.

### 5. Information you voluntarily send us

If you email us or open a GitHub Issue, we receive only what you choose to include (for example email address, message text, device details, screenshots). **GitHub Issues are public**—do not post sensitive personal or health details there.

### 6. What we do not do

- No phone number / WeChat / social login required for the core loop
- No camera-based face or attention detection
- No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs integrated by us for cross-app tracking (as of this version)
- We do **not sell** your personal information

## Purchases, subscriptions, and Apple billing

If the App Store listing offers a **paid** capability (for example multi-device **iCloud sync** as a subscription or one-time In-App Purchase):

- **Apple** processes payment, receipts, and refunds under Apple’s terms. We do not receive your full payment card number.
- Apple may share limited purchase/entitlement signals with the App (for example whether a subscription is active) so we can unlock the paid feature.
- Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings; refunds follow Apple’s process and applicable law.

Free core features (look-away rhythm and shelving, pairing plan, local diary) are intended to work **without** purchasing sync. Exact SKUs and pricing appear on the App Store product page and may vary by country or region.
