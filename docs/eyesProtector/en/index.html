EyesProtector

After near work, look farther away

An eye-strain habit coach and near-work glasses workflow companion. It nudges look-away breaks in work hours and optional dual-pair commute prompts. On Mac, the menu bar keeps pause and resume one click away.

Get & support Privacy policy

Global launch planned (China storefront may follow) · Core loop is free

Three things

No blue-light filter. No “cure myopia” training—just a more sustainable near-work day.

  • Work-hour look-aways

    Gentle prompts on interval; shelve with pause, lunch, off-work mode, or close for today.

  • Pairing plan & swaps

    Single-pair never nags about swaps; dual-pair gets commute-node prompts for near-work glasses.

  • Good-enough Smart Pause

    Focus and meeting apps can auto-shelve; plus a strain diary and adherence review.

Platforms

One SwiftUI codebase. Mac is the home base (menu bar); iPhone and iPad run the same core loop with a light UI.

  • Mac
  • iPhone
  • iPad

Important

EyesProtector is not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat, and it does not claim to restore accommodation or prevent myopia progression. Talk to an optometrist about optical plans.
