What's new

In 0.5.3

Look-away clock on the desk, smarter Mac pause, and UI in six languages.

New

Look-away clock

On an unlocked iPhone or iPad, leave the device on a desk as a foreground timer. Shares work hours and completion with the main loop. First theme, Eyes, is free; more themes are separate purchases. Mac stays on the menu bar—no clock.

New

Smart Pause on Mac

Pauses for Focus modes this Mac actually has (Work off by default). Pause apps are ones you add—no meeting presets. iPhone and iPad do not pause for Focus.

New

Menu bar, not Dock

Mac stays in the menu bar. Close the window and open it again from there.

New

Six languages

App UI follows the system: English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, and Spanish. This site matches.

New

iPhone & iPad polish

Same four tabs; iPad bottom bar swipes between pages. Device-specific guides in Settings. Comfort diary quick words when you log how your eyes feel.

Core

What we keep shipping

No blue-light filter. No “cure myopia” training—just a more sustainable near-work day.

  • Work-hour look-aways

    Gentle or strict prompts on interval; shelve with pause, off-work mode, or mute for today.

  • Pairing plan & swaps

    Single-pair never nags about swaps; dual-pair gets commute-node prompts for near-work glasses.

  • Good-enough Smart Pause

    On Mac: Focus modes you enable and frontmost apps you add. Manual pause for N minutes.

  • Comfort diary & adherence

    Evening comfort score 0–10 with quick words; look-away completion by day and by slot.
