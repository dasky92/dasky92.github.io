Import Music

Go to Settings → Import and choose audio files from the Files app or iCloud Drive.

Supported formats include MP3, FLAC, AAC, M4A, WAV, OGG, Opus, ALAC, and AIFF.

Wi-Fi Transfer

Turn on Wi-Fi Transfer in Settings and upload songs from a computer on the same network.

Keep the App on the Settings screen while uploading. Transfer stops automatically when you leave Settings.

Share from Other Apps

In Files, Safari, or other apps, tap Share and choose Verde to copy audio into your library.

You can also open an audio file with Verde from the share sheet’s Open With list.

Liked Songs

Tap the heart on any song in the player to save it to your Liked collection.

Playback and Library

  • Browse your library by Songs or Artists in the Library tab.
  • Use Search to find artists and song titles.
  • Playback modes include order, shuffle, and repeat one.
  • Lock Screen and Control Center controls are supported when music is playing.
